# Approved Playstyle Labels

These are descriptive scouting-card labels, not extra numeric ratings, special
abilities, or changes to the supplied 1–10 values. The original brief's signature
strings remain preserved in `jam_sim.h`; the visible cards use this approved set.

| Player | Proposed label | Evidence from supplied ratings | Main card ratings |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MATUS | 3PT PLAYMAKER | Three-point 10 and passing 10 make both shooting and creation central. Clutch 9 and steal 8 support a complete guard. “Top of the Key” named a court location and missed his passing strength. | Speed, three-point, passing, steal |
| MAGIC | RIM PROTECTOR | Block 10, dunk 9, power 7; speed 4 means his strength is the paint rather than chasing guards. | Speed, block, dunk, power |
| BAMBI | POWER FINISHER | Power 10 with dunk 7 and clutch 7 suggests contact finishing. Speed 4 and three-point 5 do not suggest a fast perimeter scorer. | Speed, power, dunk, passing |
| TORRES | SPEED SLASHER | Speed 10, dunk 8, passing 7 and steal 7 favor cuts, transition, and attacking the basket; three-point 3 limits outside shooting. | Speed, dunk, steal, passing |
| CHOMPA | STRETCH BIG | Three-point 8 with power 9 and block/dunk 7 supports an inside-out big. This original label already describes the ratings well. | Speed, three-point, power, block |
| MEAT | TWO-WAY SLASHER | Speed 9, steal 8 and dunk 8 support transition offense and disruptive defense; three-point 2 favors driving over jumpers. | Speed, steal, dunk, block |

Speed stays in the same position on every main card. The other three featured
skills communicate each player's useful differences. SELECT opens two detail
pages containing all eight ratings, so a lower or unfeatured rating is never
hidden from inspection. Numbers are checked against the canonical C roster by
the card generator. The user approved these six labels on October 28, 2026.
